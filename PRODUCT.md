# Portfolio — Andrei Akirov

## Users

**Primary:** Hiring managers and technical recruiters at aerospace and defense companies (Airbus, Boeing, GMV, Indra, CAE, Thales). They scan 12 tabs at once on a Thursday afternoon. They need to understand technical depth in under 10 seconds.

**Secondary:** Research supervisors or professors evaluating candidates for internships or graduate programs.

## Product Purpose

Personal engineering portfolio. Not a blog, not a resume, not a showcase of frameworks. A demonstration that Andrei builds the tools other engineers use off the shelf — from scratch, with physical understanding behind every line.

## Register

brand

## Tone

Direct. Technical without being opaque. No self-promotion language ("passionate", "driven", "experienced in"). Let the projects speak. One sentence of context per project, then numbers.

Anti-tone: modest to the point of being invisible. These are real results — state them plainly.

## Brand Voice Words

Precise. Instrumental. Controlled.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS landing pages with hero-metric templates
- "Creative portfolio" with pastel cards and emoji headers
- Gradient text on every heading
- Full-page centered hero with icon grid below

## Strategic Principles

1. Optimizador (CFD) is the lead project — it demonstrates the deepest technical ownership
2. Every project shows a number, not just a name
3. Screenshots are engineering artifacts, not decorations — they should show actual simulation output, actual UI, actual data
4. Contact info is present but not dominant — the work is the pitch
